\textbf{Key observation}: WMTP provides consistent improvements on \textbf{out-of-domain} benchmarks (HumanEval, MBPP, GSM8K) but shows decline on \textbf{in-domain} CodeContests. We analyze potential causes in Section~\ref{sec:analysis}.

\subsection{Training Diagnostics}

\subsubsection{Weight Statistics}

Table~\ref{tab:weight_stats} reports token weight statistics from the final Verifiable training run, providing insights into how the advantage weighting behaves.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Token weight statistics from WMTP training (run: \texttt{last-final-verifiable}). The clipping ratio of 17.9\% indicates that extreme weights are being controlled effectively.}
\label{tab:weight_stats}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
\textbf{Metric} & \textbf{Value} & \textbf{Interpretation} \\
\midrule
weight\_mean & 1.328 & Average upweighting (mean $> 1$) \\
weight\_std & 0.981 & Moderate variance \\
weight\_min & 0.100 & Clipping lower bound active \\
weight\_max & 3.000 & Clipping upper bound active \\
\textbf{clipping\_ratio} & \textbf{17.9\%} & Extreme value control \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Observations}:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Mean $>$ 1}: On average, tokens receive slight upweighting compared to uniform
    \item \textbf{Clipping ratio 17.9\%}: Approximately 1 in 5 tokens hits the clipping bounds, indicating effective extreme value control while preserving meaningful weight variation
\end{itemize}
